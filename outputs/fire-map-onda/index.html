How to read this view

The yellow outline is the area of interest from the shared Google My Maps layer. Each orange-to-red point is a nearby VIIRS active-fire detection; white-rimmed points fall inside the outline. Compare their positions with the satellite image to assess the likely affected area. A detection is a 375 m pixel, not a mapped fire perimeter: do not interpret the dots alone as the exact boundary of a wildfire. The imagery date may differ from the detection time.
