Read these trends with three caveats. They can each move a line without anything changing on the ground.
  • Country range. 33 plans in 2023 against 23 in 2026 — most of the fall in total need is countries leaving the set, not need declining. Switch the scope toggle to compare like with like.
  • Definition of reach. The 2023 dashboard headline used People reached (81.0M); 2024 onward use Max people reached (48.7M in 2024). On the common definition 2023 is 58.9M, so part of the 2023→2024 drop is the measure, not the response.
  • Period. 2026 covers January–April only and is marked YTD throughout. It is not comparable to a full year.
