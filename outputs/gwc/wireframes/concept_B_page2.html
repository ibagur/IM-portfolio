Why the headline and the like-for-like line disagree. Across all plans, people in need falls 188.3M → 137.5M (−27%). Across the 23 countries present in every cycle it is 143.6M → 137.5M (−4%) — so most of the headline fall is ten countries leaving the plan set, not need receding. Funding received falls ~70% on both scopes, so that decline is real. Two further breaks: the reach measure changed after 2023 (People reached 81.0M → Max people reached 58.9M on the common basis), and 2026 covers January–April only.
