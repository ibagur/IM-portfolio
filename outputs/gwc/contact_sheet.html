  1. Country focus. Page 2 gains a Global / Single country sub-tab and country slicers. The third screen shows the country state, using Sudan. In Power BI this is one bookmark on the same page, not a new page.

Three things the data says, whichever concept we pick.
  • The 27% fall in people in need is mostly composition. Across the 23 countries that appealed in every cycle, need went 143.6M → 137.5M (−4%). Ten countries left the plan set. Both scopes are on every page-2 concept via a toggle.
  • The funding collapse is real. Received fell ~70% on both scopes; the share funded went 42% → 12%. This is the defensible advocacy line.
  • The reach measure changed after 2023. The 2023 headline used People reached (81.0M); 2024 onward use Max people reached. On the common basis 2023 is 58.9M, so part of the 2023→2024 drop is definitional.
Four data issues to decide on before we build
  • Mali's country code cell contains 41053442 (its funding-requested figure) instead of MLI. Lebanon is coded LEB, Malawi MLW — neither is a valid ISO3, which breaks year-on-year joins.
  • Max people reached returns 0 for Chad, CAR, Somalia and Yemen although each has a reported WASH reach figure — this is why 2026 shows "N/A".
  • The hygiene progress bar on the live 2026 dashboard (52.3%) cannot be reproduced from the tracker, which gives 8.7%. Water (13.9%) and sanitation (10.2%) reproduce exactly.
  • Prioritized PiN severity 4–5, prioritized target and prioritized funding are not collected. They sit on the GHO monitoring dashboard and are shown as "to collect" placeholders.
